03 / Multimodal AI · Private engineering system

Multimodal AI Engine for E-commerce

An AI-assisted decision system I built for my own active e-commerce business. It combines continuous data collection, Gemini image analysis and deterministic economic models to identify and prioritise commercial opportunities.

Used in my e-commerce operations · Ongoing development

Node.js · Gemini Vision · Playwright · SQLite · Discord APIs

Continuous collection and deduplication turn changing e-commerce signals into structured candidate data.

Built and used as part of day-to-day business operations: automated information intake, deduplication, classification, selective visual analysis, profit and ROI calculations, risk checks and decision-focused alerts.

Explore AI architecture & safeguards

Selective multimodal AI. Gemini supports ambiguous text classification and image-based evidence, with structured outputs, confidence labels, request budgets and validation. AI output does not independently determine financial actions.

Deterministic decisions. Policy-bound calculations evaluate total costs, expected margins, ROI, thresholds and risk scenarios. Unsupported or contradictory inputs stay in human review rather than becoming fabricated certainty.

Reliability. SQLite provides durable state and history; scheduling is separated from processing; duplicate suppression, bounded retries, backoff and persisted notification intents support recoverable operation.

Supports a real, operating e-commerce business; selected advanced negotiation and transaction features remain subject to separate controls and validation. Commercial sources and product categories are deliberately kept private.
